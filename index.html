Short version: ViewTVY combines live TV, VOD, sports, EPG and multi-device compatibility in subscription plans priced mainly by duration and the number of simultaneous streams.

IPTV Terms Explained: VOD, EPG, PPV and Xtream Codes

IPTV

Internet Protocol Television delivers television streams through an internet connection rather than a cable or satellite signal.

VOD

Video on Demand means movies, series and other titles that can be opened when you choose instead of following a live schedule.

EPG

Electronic Program Guide is the on-screen TV schedule that shows current and upcoming programming inside compatible IPTV players.

PPV

Pay-Per-View usually refers to premium live events such as major combat-sports or special-event broadcasts. ViewTVY currently states that PPV and sports events are included in its listed tiers.

Xtream Codes Login

A common IPTV login format using a server URL, username and password. Compatible apps use these credentials to load channels, VOD and EPG data.

M3U Playlist

An alternative playlist format supported by many IPTV players. It can be used when a compatible service provides an M3U URL.

What Does “1 Connection” Mean?

One connection means one active stream at a time. It does not simply mean that you may install the IPTV player on only one device.

For example, if ViewTVY is installed on a living-room Firestick and a bedroom Smart TV, a one-connection plan is suitable if only one of those devices is streaming at any given moment. If both need to play different streams simultaneously, a two-connection plan is the more appropriate choice.

Simple rule: count the maximum number of screens that need to stream at the same time. That number is the connection count you should compare on the pricing page.

Potential Limitations to Consider

Even when a pricing plan looks attractive, IPTV services can have limitations that are worth considering before a long-term purchase.

  • Channel availability can change: a large advertised lineup does not guarantee every channel will remain available permanently.
  • Performance varies by location and network: buffering can come from local Wi-Fi, ISP routing, device performance or the source stream.
  • 4K is not universal: not every channel or VOD title will necessarily be available in 4K.
  • Third-party player costs: some IPTV apps have their own one-time or recurring fee.
  • Long plans increase commitment: the cheapest effective monthly price also requires the largest upfront payment.
